# ScanKart Demo Script (3 Minutes)

## 1. Introduction (30s)
- **Open Landing Page**: Show the clean, mobile-first UI.
- **Highlight QR Code**: Explain the "Scan to Open" concept for schools.
- **Value Prop**: "A safe, school-specific marketplace for students."

## 2. Buying Flow (1m)
- **Click 'Buy'**: Show the grid of items.
- **Filter**: Select "Books" category or search for "Math".
- **View Item**: Click on an item. Show details (Condition, Seller School).
- **WhatsApp CTA**: Click "Chat on WhatsApp" to show the pre-filled message.

## 3. Selling Flow (1m)
- **Register/Login**: Use phone `9999999999` (Admin) or register a new student.
- **Click 'Sell'**: Fill the form.
    - Name: "Science Lab Coat"
    - Price: 250
    - Upload Image: Use a sample image.
- **Submit**: Show the toast/redirect.
- **Verify**: Go back to 'Buy' page and see the new item at the top.

## 4. Admin & Conclusion (30s)
- **Admin Dashboard**: Go to `/admin/dashboard`.
- **Moderation**: Show stats and the "Remove" button for listings.
- **Poster**: Show `/poster` page for printing.
- **Wrap up**: "Ready to deploy, secure, and scalable."
